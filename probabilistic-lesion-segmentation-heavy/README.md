# Probabilistic Lesion Segmentation

Professor-aligned heavy benchmark for Abdullah-Al-Zubaer Imran. It simulates
ambiguous lesions and seven heterogeneous annotators, then compares unweighted
majority consensus with reliability-weighted probabilistic consensus across five
seeds. Dice, Brier score and boundary-focused uncertainty are reported.

This is a synthetic methodological benchmark, not a reproduction of the
professor's model or a clinical claim.

Validated five-seed result: reliability-weighted consensus improved Dice from
**0.8902 to 0.9348** (+0.0446) and reduced Brier score from **0.0815 to 0.0085**.
